About

I am a writer, producer, editor, videographer, host, and pianist based in Brooklyn, New York. I turn complex ideas into simple media that help people think and feel deeply.

Since 2021, I have worked at NBC News producing national TV on both cable and streaming. Before that, I worked in podcasts, political offices, social media, sports radio, literary festivals and more. I love archival footage, and launched an original series highlighting moments from a century of NBC broadcasts. I have a lifelong passion for soccer, and have reported on its rise in America for years. My other areas of expertise include politics, economics, international relations, music, immigration, education, and all major sports. I studied Political Economy and minored in Classical Studies at Columbia University. Below are selected samples of my work. You can view my resume via the dropdown menu at the top left.
